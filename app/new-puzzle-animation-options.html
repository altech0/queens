New Puzzle — Transition Ideas

Click "Try it" on each card to preview. Pick whichever feels right.

A — Fade to background
Dissolve through background

The grid fades out into the app background colour, spinner appears briefly, new grid fades in. Minimal, calm — similar to Safari tab switching.

B — Cell scatter
Cells dissolve out, then assemble

Each cell fades and shrinks out in a fast stagger wave. The new puzzle's cells assemble back in. Subtle — you feel the puzzle being replaced without a hard cut.

C — Scale + blur out/in
Shrink away, bloom back in

Grid scales down and blurs out (like pressing an app icon), spinner shows, new puzzle blooms back up slightly overshooting for a springy feel.

D — Slide transition
Old slides up, new slides in

Current grid slides up and off-screen while the new one slides in from below. Clear sense of forward momentum. Feels like turning a page.

E — Drop in
Old sweeps away, new drops down

The old grid rotates slightly and fades up as if whisked away, then the new one drops in with a soft blur. More playful — suits a puzzle game aesthetic.
