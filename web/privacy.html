Privacy Policy

Queens · Last updated: May 2026

Queens does not track you, profile you, show you ads, or sell your data. There are no in-app purchases. This is just a puzzle app — nothing more.

What We Store

To use Queens you choose a nickname. That nickname, along with a randomly generated authentication token, is stored on our servers so the app can fetch puzzles securely. That is the full extent of what we store.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, device ID, or any other personal information.

What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising — none, ever
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions
  • No data linked to your identity beyond a nickname you chose yourself

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your nickname and auth token are stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen inside the app. This permanently removes your nickname and auth token from our servers.
