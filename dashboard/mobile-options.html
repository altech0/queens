How to Play
Objective
Place stars so each row, column and region has exactly the required number.
No Touching Stars
Stars cannot touch each other, not even diagonally.
Controls
Tap once for ✕ mark · tap again to place ★ · tap again to clear.
Tips
Start with regions that have limited placement options. Use ✕ to eliminate impossible cells.
